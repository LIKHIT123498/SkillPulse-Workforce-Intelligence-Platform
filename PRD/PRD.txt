# 1. Executive Summary

## 1.1 Product Overview

SkillPulse is an AI-powered Labour Market Intelligence and Curriculum Alignment Platform designed to bridge the gap between industry skill requirements and existing skill-development programmes.

The platform collects and analyses labour-market signals, including job postings, employer surveys, industry requirements, emerging technologies and training programme data.

Using Natural Language Processing (NLP), Machine Learning (ML) and data analytics, SkillPulse identifies:

- Skills currently demanded by industries.
- Emerging and declining skill requirements.
- Differences between industry expectations and existing course curricula.
- Skill shortages across geographical locations.
- Training programmes that require modification or modernization.
- Potential curriculum improvements based on employment trends.

The platform translates these insights into actionable recommendations for training institutions, government administrators, employers and candidates.

## 1.2 Problem Statement

Traditional skill-development programmes often rely on historical occupation classifications and outdated industry requirements.

This creates several problems:

1. Course curricula may not reflect current industry technologies.
2. Training institutions may continue offering courses with declining employment demand.
3. Employers struggle to find candidates with relevant competencies.
4. Students may complete training programmes without acquiring industry-relevant skills.
5. Government authorities lack a continuous, evidence-based mechanism to plan district-level training requirements.

SkillPulse aims to address these challenges through a centralized, data-driven intelligence system.

## 1.3 Product Vision

To create a continuous intelligence ecosystem that connects labour-market demand, skill-development programmes, educational institutions and employment opportunities through data-driven insights.

# 4. Functional Requirements

The product is divided into five primary functional modules.

## MODULE 1: Labour Market Intelligence

**Priority: P0 – Core MVP**

### Purpose

Collect, process and analyse job-market information to understand current and emerging industry requirements.

### Functional Requirements

FR-101: The system shall support importing job-posting datasets through CSV files.

FR-102: The system shall store job title, company, location, sector, description, experience requirements and associated skills wherever available.

FR-103: The system shall preprocess job descriptions by removing unnecessary formatting, duplicate content and irrelevant text.

FR-104: The system shall extract technical skills, tools, technologies and competencies from job descriptions using NLP.

FR-105: The system shall normalize equivalent skill names into standardized skill identifiers.

FR-106: The system shall calculate skill-demand frequency across job roles and locations.

FR-107: The system shall identify emerging and declining skill-demand patterns when sufficient historical data is available.

### Expected Output

A labour-market intelligence dashboard containing:

- Most demanded technical skills.
- Emerging technologies.
- Skill demand by job role.
- Skill demand by industry sector.
- Geographical demand distribution.
- Experience and proficiency requirements.

---

## MODULE 2: Skill Gap Analysis

**Priority: P0 – Core MVP**

### Purpose

Compare industry skill requirements against the competencies covered by existing training programmes.

### Functional Requirements

FR-201: The system shall maintain a structured skill taxonomy.

FR-202: The system shall maintain a database of available courses and their associated skills.

FR-203: The system shall compare industry-required skills with course-covered skills.

FR-204: The system shall identify missing, partially covered and adequately covered competencies.

FR-205: The system shall calculate a skill-alignment score for each course.

FR-206: The system shall generate a report describing the differences between industry requirements and course curricula.

### Skill Alignment Logic

For the MVP, implement a weighted skill coverage score:

Skill Alignment Score:

(Number of weighted required skills covered / Total weighted required skills) × 100

The implementation must account for proficiency requirements and skill importance where data is available.

### Example

Suppose an industry role requires:

- Python – 25%
- SQL – 25%
- Machine Learning – 20%
- Cloud Computing – 15%
- Data Visualization – 15%

An existing course covers Python, SQL and basic Data Visualization.

The platform should identify:

- Covered: Python, SQL.
- Partially covered: Data Visualization.
- Missing: Machine Learning, Cloud Computing.

The resulting alignment score should be accompanied by the underlying skill-level explanation rather than presented as an unexplained number.

### Expected Output

A skill-gap report containing:

- Course name.
- Target job role.
- Industry-required competencies.
- Existing course competencies.
- Missing competencies.
- Skill alignment percentage.
- Recommended improvements.

---

## MODULE 3: Curriculum Recommendation Engine

**Priority: P1 – Advanced MVP**

### Purpose

Recommend curriculum modifications based on industry demand and detected skill gaps.

### Functional Requirements

FR-301: The system shall identify missing competencies in existing courses.

FR-302: The system shall prioritize missing skills based on industry demand and relevance.

FR-303: The system shall generate suggested topics and learning outcomes for curriculum improvement.

FR-304: The system shall provide explanations for every recommendation.

FR-305: The system shall allow authorized administrators to review, approve or reject recommendations.

FR-306: The system shall retain the original curriculum and proposed modifications separately.

### Recommendation Engine

The recommendation process should combine:

1. Skill-demand frequency.
2. Industry relevance.
3. Course alignment score.
4. Emerging technology trends.
5. Employer feedback.
6. Skill proficiency requirements.

An LLM can generate human-readable curriculum suggestions using structured analytical results.

**Important:** The LLM must not independently determine labour-market demand. Numerical demand calculations must come from verified data and analytical pipelines.

### Expected Output

Example:

Existing Course: Diploma in Computer Applications

Identified Gap: Limited coverage of modern data analytics tools.

Suggested Improvements:
- Introduce Python for Data Analysis.
- Add SQL-based data management.
- Include practical data visualization exercises.
- Introduce introductory cloud computing concepts.

These are illustrative recommendations, not validated curriculum changes.

# 8. Database Design Requirements

The following entities form the initial relational data model.

| Entity | Important attributes |
|---|---|
| Users | id, name, email, password_hash, role |
| Districts | id, district_name, state |
| Companies | id, company_name, industry_id |
| Industries | id, industry_name |
| JobPostings | id, title, description, company_id, district_id, posted_date |
| Skills | id, skill_name, category, canonical_name |
| JobSkills | id, job_id, skill_id, importance, proficiency |
| Courses | id, course_name, qualification, institution_id |
| CourseSkills | id, course_id, skill_id, coverage_level |
| SkillGapReports | id, course_id, target_role, alignment_score |
| Recommendations | id, course_id, skill_id, recommendation_text, status |
| EmployerSurveys | id, employer_id, job_role, submitted_at |
| SurveySkills | id, survey_id, skill_id, proficiency |
| CandidateProfiles | id, user_id, district_id |
| CandidateSkills | id, candidate_id, skill_id, proficiency |

### Database Design Rules

1. Use UUID or another consistent primary-key strategy.
2. Establish foreign-key relationships.
3. Add indexes to frequently queried columns.
4. Prevent duplicate canonical skills.
5. Store original imported job descriptions for traceability.
6. Track dataset source and import timestamps.
7. Keep generated recommendations separate from approved curriculum changes.
8. Preserve the provenance of analytical results.

A formal ER diagram must be prepared before implementing database relationships.

- Privacy protection for candidate and employer information.

AI-generated curriculum recommendations must remain advisory until reviewed and approved by authorized human stakeholders.

The platform must not automatically classify a district or training institution as underperforming based on incomplete datasets.

# 22. Product Direction and Long-Term Vision

SkillPulse should evolve beyond a job-market analytics dashboard.

Its long-term purpose is to establish a continuous feedback loop between industry demand and skill-development programmes.

The intended cycle is:

**Industry Demand → Skill Identification → Gap Analysis → Curriculum Modernization → Training Delivery → Placement Outcomes → Industry Feedback**

The platform should eventually use placement outcomes and employer feedback to evaluate whether recommended curriculum changes actually improve employment readiness.

This creates an evidence-based system for continuously improving workforce development.

**Core Product Principle:**

SkillPulse should not merely tell users which skills are popular. It should explain which skills are missing, where they are missing, why they matter and what training institutions should do about them.
